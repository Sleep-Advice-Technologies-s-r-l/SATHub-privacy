Privacy Policy – SAT Hub

Last updated: November 23, 2026

1. Data Controller

This Privacy Policy describes how personal and technical data is processed in connection with the use of the SAT Hub mobile application, available for Android devices.

The application is developed and managed by:

Company: Sleep Advice Technologies S.r.l.

Registered office: Corso Rodolfo Montevecchio 46, 10129 Turin (TO), Italy

Email for privacy requests: support@satechnologies.eu

Website: https://www.satechnology.eu

For requests concerning the protection of personal data, you may contact the Data Controller at the email address provided above.

2. Purpose of the Application

SAT Hub is an application that allows authorized users to connect to compatible Garmin devices and download, install, and update Garmin Connect IQ applications through a corporate distribution and license management system.

Access to restricted features requires credentials provided by the organization managing the service.

The application does not allow users to register new accounts independently.

3. Categories of Data Processed

To provide its features, SAT Hub processes the following categories of data.

3.1 User Identifiers and Authentication Data

The application transmits the credentials entered by the user, including username and password, to the company's servers in order to verify the user's identity and authorize access to the services.

The system may also use authentication tokens to manage sessions and authorized requests.

Account identifiers are stored in the company's systems for as long as necessary to manage user accounts and their related permissions.

3.2 Garmin Device Identifiers and Technical Information

SAT Hub uses Garmin software components to discover, pair, and communicate with compatible Garmin devices via Bluetooth.

During these operations, the application and its integrated SDKs may collect and process technical information, including:

  • Unique identifiers of Garmin devices.
  • Bluetooth addresses.
  • Device name and model.
  • Software version.
  • Information about the status and availability of device services.

During Bluetooth scanning, identifiers and technical information relating to nearby Garmin devices may also be detected temporarily.

Identifiers required for license management may be transmitted to and stored in the corporate systems of Sleep Advice Technologies S.r.l.

Other technical data collected during Bluetooth communication is processed as part of the device pairing and management features.

3.3 Installation Identifiers and Push Notifications

The application uses Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM), a service provided by Google, to receive notifications about the availability of application updates.

To operate the service, Firebase may process technical identifiers relating to the app installation that are required to route and deliver notifications.

3.4 Technical Connection Data

During communications with the company's servers, technical data required for the operation of the service may be processed, including IP addresses, information about network requests, operation results, and technical information used for security purposes.

If this data is stored in server logs, it is subject to the retention periods defined by the Data Controller.

3.5 Bluetooth and Device Permissions

SAT Hub requires the Android permissions necessary to discover and communicate with compatible Garmin devices via Bluetooth.

On certain versions of Android, location permissions may be required to enable Bluetooth features.

SAT Hub does not intentionally use these permissions to track the user's geographic location.

3.6 Personal Information Requested During Device Pairing

During the pairing process for a compatible Garmin device, SAT Hub may ask the user for certain personal information, including:

  • Sex.
  • Age.
  • Height.
  • Weight.
  • Usual sleep start and end times.

In the current version of the Android application, this information is processed exclusively in the device's temporary memory during the pairing process and is not used to pair the Garmin device.

The data is not transmitted to the servers of Sleep Advice Technologies S.r.l., is not sent to Garmin devices, and is not stored persistently by the application.

The information is not used for health analysis, automated profiling, or assessments of users' personal characteristics.

The data may remain temporarily in the application's memory until the relevant state is destroyed or the process is terminated.

The information entered is not associated with corporate accounts or stored in the databases used for license management.

4. Purposes of Processing

Data is processed exclusively for purposes connected with the management and operation of the service, including:

  • Authentication and management of authorized accounts.
  • Verification of licenses and permissions.
  • Pairing Garmin devices with accounts.
  • Installation and updating of Garmin Connect IQ applications.
  • Sending technical notifications and updates.
  • Protecting systems and preventing unauthorized use.
  • Managing support requests and resolving technical issues, where applicable.

SAT Hub does not intentionally use user data for personalized advertising, commercial profiling, or the sale of personal data.

Sleep Advice Technologies S.r.l. (SAT) manages SAT Hub and the related authentication, application distribution, and license management services.

SAT determines the purposes and means of the personal data processing carried out for its own activities and acts as the Data Controller for those activities.

Client organizations are responsible for the processing activities for which they independently determine the purposes and essential means.

Where SAT and a client organization jointly determine the purposes and means of processing, their respective responsibilities are governed in accordance with Article 26 of the GDPR.

The legal bases for processing are determined in relation to the respective purposes and the roles of the parties involved.

For information and requests concerning the processing of personal data, you may contact SAT at support@satechnologies.eu.

6. Data Recipients and Third-Party Services

Data processed by SAT Hub is accessible to authorized personnel of Sleep Advice Technologies S.r.l. and to any technology providers required for the operation of the service.

The application uses the following third-party components:

  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM)

    Used to send push notifications concerning application updates. The service processes the technical identifiers required to deliver notifications.

  • Garmin Connect IQ Mobile SDK for Android

    Used to communicate with Connect IQ applications on compatible Garmin devices and to provide installation and update features.

  • Garmin Health Companion SDK for Android

    Used for pairing and Bluetooth communication with compatible Garmin devices.

    In the current SAT Hub configuration, the application does not use the health and physical activity data collection features.

During Bluetooth communication with Garmin devices, the integrated SDKs may collect device identifiers, technical information, and metadata, as well as query services concerning file status and availability.

In the current SAT Hub configuration, these features are not used to transfer health data or FIT files to the company's servers.

Connect IQ communications may involve the Garmin Connect Mobile application, which is subject to its own data processing practices.

Personal data processed by the company's systems is not disclosed to third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Any processing carried out by technology providers is subject to their respective contractual terms and privacy policies.

7. Information Security

The Data Controller adopts technical and organizational measures to protect the information processed by the application, including:

  • User authentication through personal credentials.
  • Control of permissions and licenses associated with accounts and Garmin devices.
  • Communications with the company's servers protected using encrypted protocols.
  • Access to corporate systems restricted to authorized personnel.
  • Password protection using secure hashing algorithms.

Security measures are managed and updated according to technical requirements and the risks associated with data processing.

8. Data Retention

Sleep Advice Technologies S.r.l. retains personal data for as long as necessary to manage accounts, licenses, and SAT Hub services, in compliance with applicable law.

In particular:

  • Account identifiers are retained for the duration of the account's validity and management.
  • Garmin device associations are retained until they are removed, permissions are revoked, or the account is deleted, unless further retention is justified.
  • Technical data and any logs are retained for limited periods, in accordance with company policies.
  • Data managed by third-party services is also subject to the retention policies of the respective providers.

Information concerning sex, age, height, weight, and usual sleep times is processed exclusively in the application's temporary memory and is not stored on the company's servers.

Account deletion does not necessarily result in the immediate deletion of data contained in logs, backups, or separate systems, to which the respective retention and deletion procedures apply.

9. Data Subject Rights and Data Deletion

Users may exercise the rights provided for under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), including the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, objection, and data portability, where provided for by applicable law.

Requests may be sent to support@satechnologies.eu.

Account deletion is carried out by authorized personnel of Sleep Advice Technologies S.r.l. through the company's administrative systems.

The procedure involves deleting the credentials, permissions, licenses, and Garmin device associations recorded in the corporate authentication system.

Account deletion does not automatically remove local Bluetooth pairings, data stored on Garmin devices, or information that may be retained in separate systems, logs, and backups.

Any deletion requests concerning such data are assessed and handled separately, in accordance with applicable law and the Data Controller's retention policies.

10. Children

SAT Hub is intended for authorized users of the organization and is not specifically designed for children.

The application does not offer features specifically intended for minors.
